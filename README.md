# Content Creation 101

*Everything about growing online with content, in one place: personal brand, hooks, storytelling, how attention works, going viral, video, articles, getting seen, systems, making money and writing*

We took 65 of the best videos and articles from creators who grew huge audiences, over 300,000 words in total, and turned them into one free guide. The filler is gone and every important point is kept

**11 modules · 176 lessons · 217 frameworks · 290 examples · about 25 hours of reading**

Put together by [@BuzzMalx](https://x.com/BuzzMalx). Every creator is credited at the end: [Credits](CREDITS.md)

## How to use this guide

- **read it in order** if you are just starting. Module 1 (personal brand) decides what you post about in every module after it
- **jump to one module** if you have one problem right now (no views: modules 2 and 5, not growing: module 8, no money: module 10)
- **every lesson ends with "Try it"**, a small task you can do today
- **every module ends with a cheat sheet**, the whole module on one page
- **[the Personal Brand Playbook](PLAYBOOK.md)** puts every step from the guide in order, with the questions to answer for your own brand
